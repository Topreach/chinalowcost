Admin records are available in the existing Financial activity / Reports screen (A27), restricted to owner and finance administrators.

The UTC date filter covers paid purchases, completed refunds, stock movements and accepted purchase receipts. Total customer registrations count all customer accounts; new registrations use account.created audit dates. Older missing registration dates remain unknown. Customer purchase rankings include inactive and unverified accounts and customers without purchases.

Goods totals use verified paid orders in the current payment environment. Unpaid orders are excluded. Line subtotals are before order-level discounts, shipping, tax and returns; customer spend is gross paid order value. Refunds are shown separately and do not automatically restock goods. Tables show up to 500 rows, while totals cover all matching records. Narrow the period for large histories; comprehensive pagination/export is not yet implemented.

Supplier receipt records link accepted inspection IDs to purchase orders, quantities, cost, recorder, supplier contact/location and terms. New POs snapshot supplier details; older POs use current supplier information and are labelled accordingly. This records the administrative PO/inspection match, not a verified unit-level supplier-to-customer provenance chain.

Purchase history supports promotion planning but does not establish consent to send marketing messages. This is an operational reporting view, not a complete financial ledger, tax statement or profit report.
