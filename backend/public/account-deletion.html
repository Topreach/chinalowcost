Request account deletion

ChinaLowCost customers and workers can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

In the application, open your profile's account security page and select Request account deletion. Alternatively, email support@chinalowcost.com from your registered email address with the subject “ChinaLowCost account deletion”. Do not send your password or authenticator codes.

Support will verify account ownership and review the request. Any open orders, refunds or balances will be discussed before processing. Transaction, fraud prevention and legally required records may need to be retained; support will explain the applicable retention period. Submitting a request does not immediately erase your account.
